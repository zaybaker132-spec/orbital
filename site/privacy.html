Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version: the Orbital app keeps everything you write and save on your own computer. We don't run servers that receive your notes, we don't have accounts on our side, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

The Orbital desktop app

  • Your data stays on your computer. Notes, documents, memories, projects and your Orbital account are stored in a private database in your computer's application-data folder. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes them.
  • AI is optional and uses your own key. If you add an AI provider key (for example Anthropic), the text of your question and the notes Orbital selects as context are sent to that provider to produce an answer, under your own account and that provider's privacy terms. Your key is stored in your computer's secure keychain. Without a key, nothing is sent to any AI provider.
  • Web research, when you use it, sends your search terms to the sources it searches (such as Wikipedia, OpenAlex, or your AI provider's web search).
  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub, where Orbital's downloads are published, whether a newer version exists. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens, as with any download.
  • Weather in the sidebar is optional. If you choose a place, its name (while you search) and its coordinates, rounded to about a kilometre, are sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to get the forecast. Nothing else is sent, and no weather is fetched until you pick a place.
  • Meetings connect people directly (peer to peer). To find a route between computers, the app contacts public connection servers (STUN), which see your IP address.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the download files by GitHub. GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you load the page.
  • To show the latest version, your browser asks GitHub's public API for the newest release.

Children

Orbital isn't directed at children under 13.
